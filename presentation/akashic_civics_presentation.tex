\section{The Civic Problem}

Local criminal justice decisions shape lives -- but are rarely measured:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Arrests, charges, convictions, and sentences happen at the local level
    \item District attorneys, sheriffs, and judges wield enormous discretion
    \item Disparity by race, income, and neighborhood is well-documented nationally -- but local data is fragmented
    \item Without measurable baselines, communities cannot advocate for change or evaluate reform
\end{itemize}

\section{What Akashic Civics Does}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Ingest local case data from any jurisdiction
    \item Run statistical tests (Z-tests, regression, causal inference)
    \item Track disparities over time with streaming analysis
    \item Export reports for advocacy, journalism, and policy
    \item Available in TypeScript, Python, and R
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Implementation choice:} Doubly-robust methods protect against model misspecification.

\section{Design Principles}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Multi-language by design -- Meet users where they are (TS, Python, R)
    \item Statistical rigor first -- No headline numbers without CI and effect sizes
    \item Local data, local control -- Data never leaves user's machine unless they choose
    \item Composable, not monolithic -- Use dashboard, CLI, or just the R package
\end{enumerate}

\section{Why Doubly-Robust? Why Bayesian?}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Simple rate comparisons $\rightarrow$ confounded by case mix
    \item Doubly-robust $\rightarrow$ consistent if either propensity or outcome model is correct
    \item Bayesian hierarchical $\rightarrow$ small jurisdictions get stable estimates through partial pooling
\end{itemize}

\section{Why Stream, Why Alert?}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Batch analysis answers ``what happened?'' -- Streaming answers ``is it happening right now?''
    \item Alert thresholds create early warning for advocates and oversight bodies
\end{itemize}

\section{A Concrete Scenario}

Mid-sized county, 12,000 cases/year:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Community loads 3 years of case data into Akashic Civics
    \item Doubly-robust: Black defendants convicted at 1.4$\times$ rate of White defendants ($p < 0.01$)
    \item Streaming alert: disparity ratio rose from 1.2$\times$ to 1.6$\times$ after new DA
    \item Community presents at hearing -> DA adopts new charging guidelines
    \item Next quarter: disparity drops to 1.1$\times$
\end{itemize}

\section{Promoting Democracy}
